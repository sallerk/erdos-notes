## 2. The three that matter most

**D01 is the worst thing in the directory**, because it is published and because it is the
one defect whose claim, if it were true, would settle the problem. `NOTE.md` states as
established fact that *every* exact solution of *all fifteen* patterns is four-concyclic.
That sentence implies h(5) >= 4, hence h(5) = 4, two rows below a table that says h(5) = 4 is
NOT ESTABLISHED. Its evidence is `r2check.py`: 120 random `least_squares` starts per pattern,
anything under residual 1e-10 counted as an "exact root", 577 of them found. That is a
numerical sample of a solution set, and the suite cannot catch the overstatement because
`verify.py` encodes the same wording in a PASS label. It is in the published copy and in the
unposted draft comment as well.

**D02 breaks the new section's logic as written.** Section 5c says there are six placements,
lists six, excludes all six, and then announces a survivor. The survivor is the crossed
pairing, which is none of the six (item 5 is the *opposite*, matched pairing). There are
seven. The mathematics is fine and the branch really is closed; the case list as printed does
not get the reader there.

**D14 is the calibration for everything else.** The first sentence of the note says every
defect from the 2026-09-07 audit was fixed or explicitly withdrawn, and names three reports.
There is a fourth, `AUDIT_ROUND2.md`, dated the same day, with 20 findings, named nowhere; at
least six of them are still open today.

## 3. Defects, ranked

### D01. A universal statement about the solution sets of fifteen polynomial systems is published as est [CRITICAL]
**Where.** NOTE.md:372 (blockquote); publish/p831/NOTE.md:347-348; publish/p831/verify.py:239; DRAFT_COMMENT.md:25
**What is wrong.** A universal statement about the solution sets of fifteen polynomial systems is published as established fact on the strength of a finite numerical sample. NOTE.md and the published copy both carry the blockquote "Every exact solution of every one of the fifteen surviving patterns places four of the five points on a common circle", introduced as "the correct statement". **Fix.** Restate as what the artifact shows: 577 numerically converged solutions from 1800 random starts across the fifteen patterns, every one four-concyclic to within 2.65e-12 on a scale of 1, which is numerical evidence. Fix all four sites in one pass, including verify.py's check label ("every root FOUND by r2check is four-concyclic (numerical, 577 roots from 1800 starts)"), and do not post the draft comment until its clause is corrected.
